About the Project

This project was conducted as part of the TRM 541.01 Cultural Heritage Tourism course under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Evinç Doğan. The study focuses on Eyüpsultan, İstanbul, a district known for its rich historical, cultural, and religious heritage.

The study was carried out through a sensewalk along a predefined route connecting key landmarks, public spaces, commercial areas, and historic streets. By following this route on foot, the project explores how sensory experiences change across space and contribute to the overall sense of place.

Drawing on the concepts of urban sensescapes and Non-Representational Theory (NRT), observations were recorded through seven layers:

  • Visual Layers → Historic vs Contemporary Landscapes
  • Material Layers → Textures, Surfaces, and Urban Fabric
  • Acoustic Layers → Calls, Conversations and Soundscapes
  • Olfactory Layers → Scents of Memory and Ritual
  • Gustatory Layers → Culinary Traces and Local Flavours
  • Behavioral Layers → Everyday Performances and Ritual Practices
  • Density Layers → Flows, Gatherings and Urban Intensity

With these layers, it is aimed to capture how Eyüpsultan is experienced through everyday practices, movement, and sensory encounters.
